# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Criticise code, not the person who wrote it.

## What we expect

battlestack is maintained by SevenLab and open to anybody who wants to
contribute. That works when discussion stays about the work:

- Assume the person you are replying to is competent and acting in good faith.
- Say what you think is wrong and why. "This breaks X because Y" is useful;
  "this is a mess" is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline your change. A no with a reason is a
  legitimate answer, not an insult.
- Be patient with people who are new to this. Everybody was, recently enough.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on. Nobody keeps score.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, reopening settled decisions to make a
  point, pursuing someone across issues.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues, pull requests and discussions, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Email [hello@sevenlab.ai](mailto:hello@sevenlab.ai). Reports go to the
maintainers and stay confidential.

Tell us what happened and where. You do not need to have a solution in mind or
be certain it "counts". If you are unsure whether something crosses a line, that
is a reasonable thing to ask about.

We will look into it and act, which may mean a private word, editing or removing
content, or blocking someone from the project. We will not discuss the details
of a report with people who are not involved.

Maintainers are held to this as well. A report about a maintainer goes to the
same address and is taken as seriously as any other.
